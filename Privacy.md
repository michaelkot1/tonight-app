# Privacy Policy for Tonight

**Last updated:** September 17, 2026

Tonight (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is a social “what to watch” app that helps you and your friends pick movies and TV shows. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

By using Tonight, you agree to this policy.

## Information We Collect

### Account information
When you create an account, we may collect:
- Email address and password (if you use email sign-in)
- Information from Sign in with Apple (such as a name or email Apple provides, including a private relay email if you hide your email)
- A unique user ID for your account

### Profile information
- Username / handle
- Display name
- Avatar (including generated avatar options you choose)

### App activity
- Title ratings (e.g. loved / liked / meh)
- Saved titles
- Streaming services you say you subscribe to
- Friend connections and invite activity
- Decider preferences (who you’re watching with, filters such as movie/TV/genre)

### Contacts (optional)
If you allow Contacts access to find friends, we may read contact email addresses from your device and send them to our servers **only to match people who already use Tonight**. We do not sell your contacts. You can deny or revoke this permission in system settings.

### Device and technical data
- Basic technical data needed to run the service (e.g. app version, authentication tokens stored securely on device)
- We do **not** currently use third-party advertising trackers or sell personal information for ads

We do **not** collect precise location for the core product.

## How We Use Information
We use this information to:
- Create and manage your account
- Provide matching, friends, Decider recommendations, search, and saves
- Show movie/TV metadata (posters, synopses, ratings) from third-party providers
- Improve reliability and security
- Respond to support requests
- Comply with law

## Third-Party Services
We use trusted processors to operate Tonight, including:
- **Supabase** — authentication, database, and related backend services
- **The Movie Database (TMDB)** — movie and TV metadata and images
- **Apple** — Sign in with Apple and App Store distribution

Their use of information is also governed by their own privacy policies.

## Sharing
We do **not** sell your personal information.

We may share information:
- With service providers who help us operate the app (under obligations to protect it)
- With other Tonight users as part of the product (e.g. your handle, avatar, and ratings visible to friends/features that require them)
- If required by law, or to protect rights, safety, and security
- In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (with notice where required)

## Data Retention
We keep your information while your account is active and as needed to provide the service. If you delete your account, we delete or de-identify associated personal data from our systems, subject to limited retention where legally required (e.g. security logs, fraud prevention).

## Your Choices and Rights
- **Access / update:** You can update profile details in the app.
- **Contacts:** Optional; controllable in iOS Settings.
- **Delete account:** Use **Delete account** in Profile. This requests full remote deletion of your account and associated app data.
- **Sign out:** Available in Profile.
- Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (access, deletion, etc.). Contact us to exercise them.

## Children’s Privacy
Tonight is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

## Security
We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information (including encrypted transport via HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## International Users
Our service may be hosted or processed in the United States or other countries where our providers operate. By using Tonight, you understand your information may be transferred to those locations.

## Changes
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
